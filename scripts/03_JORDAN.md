# 03 — JORDAN (Nike Air Jordan)
## "GRAVITY IS A MYTH"
### Cinematic 3D Scroll Experience Script

---

## STEP 1: DEEP RESEARCH — ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS

### Most Successful Moments (Evidence-Based)
- **Air Jordan 1 (1985 NBA Ban):** Nike paid Michael Jordan's $5,000/game fines to keep him wearing banned black/red shoes. The controversy became the marketing. Every Jordan release still carries that rebellious DNA.
- **Air Jordan 3 (1988):** Tinker Hatfield designed the elephant print, visible Air unit, and the iconic elephant print. Jordan threatened to leave Nike — Hatfield saved the brand relationship. The shoe is why Jordan stayed.
- **The Jumpman Logo:** Derived from a 1984 Jacobus photo of Jordan doing a grand jeté. Not a dunk — a ballet leap. Jordan is simultaneously athletic AND artistic.
- **Space Jam (1996):** The film was essentially a 90-minute Jordan Brand commercial. Sold $1.2B in merchandise. Proved Jordan could transcend sport into mythology.
- **"Banned" Commercial (1985):** 45-second TV spot. Commissioner's voice-over. Banned shoe. Created the template for every hypebeast release since.
- **Bred/Chicago Colorway:** Black/red and black/white/red become cultural touchstones. Every retro release causes lines around the block. **Scarcity + nostalgia = infinite demand.**
- **Travis Scott x Jordan 1 (2019):** Backwards Swoosh. Pockets. Sold for 10x retail immediately. Jordan Brand in 2019 understood: **subvert the icon to keep it alive.**

### What Made It Work
- **Emotional Trigger:** Aspiration + scarcity + legend. Jordan shoes don't just make you fast — they make you mythological. Every wearer becomes a participant in the legend.
- **Cultural Positioning:** The apex of sneaker culture. The benchmark by which all other shoes are judged. Jordan isn't a shoe brand — it's a **canon of American mythology.**
- **Audience Psychology:** Jordan buyers believe the shoe contains Jordan's greatness. Magical thinking, but consistent and universal. The Air unit literally looks like "captured air" — a floating piece of physics.
- **Key Insight:** The brand's power source is **one man's mythological status**, expressed through color, silhouette, and scarcity. The moment Jordan becomes ordinary is the moment the brand dies. So they feed the myth constantly.

### Visual Identity
- Jumpman logo: mid-air, arms extended, gravity suspended
- Black / Red / White as the eternal triumvirate
- Wings logo (the original): angelic, aspirational
- Elephant print: unexpected luxury, shows Jordan's range
- Photography: court-level, extreme close-up on shoes mid-play, motion blur

### Core Narrative Archetype
**The Living Legend** — Jordan doesn't just play the game; he defies its fundamental constraints. Gravity. Rules. Limits. The shoe captures that defiance in wearable form.

## STEP 2: EXPERIENCE STRATEGY

### Concept: "The Moment Before Landing"

The entire experience takes place in one frozen moment: Michael Jordan in the air. The user scrolls through SPACE and TIME while Jordan is suspended at peak altitude. Each scene reveals a different dimension of the legend. The shoe exists in zero gravity — because it was built to never come down.

Why this works:
- The Jumpman is the single most powerful brand image in footwear. Making the ENTIRE website exist within that one moment is cinematic genius.
- It reflects the real psychological experience of watching Jordan play: time seemed to slow when he was in the air.
- Technically spectacular: an experience that takes place entirely within a single suspended moment is unique, memorable, and instantly shareable.

## STEP 3: CINEMATIC DIRECTOR-STYLE SCRIPT

---

### SCENE 1 — "THE JUMP" (TAKEOFF)
**Scroll Range:** 0–12%

**Environment:**
Chicago Stadium floor. But stylized: the court is photorealistic, the crowd is rendered as millions of warm light particles. Above: pure black. The ambient temperature of the space is tense — 4th quarter, 0.4 seconds remaining. **The kind of silence that precedes a legend.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Start position: floor level, looking up at the foul line
- Lens: 14mm extreme wide — the court feels cathedral-scale
- Camera begins SLOW tilting upward as Jordan's legs enter frame from below
- Speed: glacially slow. This is 0.1x speed — time dilation begins immediately

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Jordan enters frame: feet visible first, then legs, then full silhouette
- Every element in slow motion: sweat droplets hang in air, sneakers catch court light
- Air Jordan 1 "Bred" colorway — the red practically glows against the dark court
- At 8% scroll: Jordan reaches maximum altitude — FREEZE. Complete stillness.

**Transition:**
Complete freeze → camera begins moving AROUND the frozen Jordan figure → Scene 2

**Visual Effects:**
- Motion blur on Jordan until freeze point
- Sweat droplet simulation: 2k particles, physics-based, frozen mid-air
- Court: realistic wood texture with procedural scuff marks
- Crowd light particles: 2M points in stadium bowl shape, slight chromatic variance

**Interaction Details:**
- Scroll immediately triggers time dilation: slower scroll = slower motion
- At freeze point: mouse movement causes camera to orbit frozen figure (no scroll needed)
- Clicking Jordan's shoe at freeze: zoom to shoe detail, text appears: "1985. They said you couldn't. You flew."

**Sound Design:**
- Stadium crowd in slow-motion (pitch-shifted down 60%)
- Heartbeat: 80bpm slowing to 40bpm
- Air pressure sound as Jordan rises
- At freeze: total silence. For 2 full seconds.

### SCENE 2 — "THE VIEW FROM THE TOP"
**Scroll Range:** 12–28%

**Environment:**
Camera is now level with Jordan — both floating at peak altitude. The stadium BELOW. The floor is tiny. The crowd below is a sea of light. Above: the stadium rafters, championship banners, then ceiling. The perspective makes the user feel like they too are suspended. **This is what it feels like to be Jordan.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Camera at exact Jordan eye level: 2.4m above court
- First-person-adjacent: slightly behind Jordan's right shoulder
- Slow 180° orbit over 10 scroll-seconds — the full panorama from up here
- Lens: 28mm — slightly wide, gives spatial awareness
- Constant: camera tracks Jordan's chest (pivot point) during orbit

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Jordan's body in ultra-detailed slow motion: muscle tension, fabric movement, shoe laces trailing
- Air Jordan 3 materializes in front of camera (the shoe that saved everything)
- AJ3 rotates slowly, elephant print visible — tactile detail pushes close
- Behind Jordan: ghost silhouettes of every other Jordan shoe silhouette in history — floating like spirits

**Transition:**
The ghost silhouettes converge, then scatter outward as shooting stars → Scene 3

**Visual Effects:**
- Hero character: cloth simulation on jersey, shoe laces use Verlet springs
- Stadium depth: 5-layer depth fog separating floor, crowd, rafters, ceiling
- Ghost silhouettes: translucent shader with inner glow (emissive edge)
- Star scatter: particle burst with velocity trails

**Interaction Details:**
- Mouse orbit speed: user can control orbit speed with mouse velocity
- Hover ghost silhouette: shoe name + year appears, silhouette solidifies
- Scroll pause: Jordan slowly breathes — the only movement in a frozen world

**Sound Design:**
- Wind at altitude (slightly whistling)
- Echo reverb on everything — space feels enormous
- Each ghost shoe appearance: brief chime, different tone per shoe

## STEP 5: TECHNICAL TRANSLATION

### Stack
```
Framework:    React Three Fiber + @react-three/drei
Animation:    GSAP ScrollTrigger (primary) + custom RAF loop
Post-FX:      MotionBlur, Bloom, DepthOfField, Vignette
Physics:      Rapier for landing shockwave
Audio:        Howler.js (positional audio for court scenes)
```

### Key Techniques

**Time Dilation System:**
```javascript
// Map scroll progress to time scale
const timeScale = useRef(0.1); // Jordan is always at 0.1x speed
useFrame((state, delta) => {
  const adjustedDelta = delta * timeScale.current * scrollVelocityMultiplier;
  jordan.current.update(adjustedDelta);
});
```

**Freeze-and-Orbit (Scene 1 → 2 transition):**
```javascript
// When scrollProgress hits 0.08, lock Jordan position
// Enable mouseOrbit mode: camera moves, Jordan stays
if (scrollProgress >= 0.08 && scrollProgress < 0.12) {
  enableMouseOrbit(jordan.current.position);
}
```

**Patent Leather Mirror Material (Scene 4):**
```javascript
const material = new THREE.MeshStandardMaterial({
  metalness: 1.0,
  roughness: 0.02,
  envMapIntensity: 3.0,
  envMap: cubeRenderTarget.texture, // real-time cube camera
});
```

**Wings Logo Particle Formation:**
- Pre-compute logo pixel positions offline
- Runtime: particles in random positions, physics simulation pulls them toward logo positions
- GPGPU compute shader for 200k particle updates per frame
- Spring constant tuned so formation takes exactly 2 seconds

**Floor Shockwave Shader:**
```glsl
float ring = abs(distance(uv, center) - uTime * 0.3);
float ripple = 1.0 - smoothstep(0.0, 0.02, ring);
gl_FragColor = vec4(vec3(1.0) * ripple, 1.0);
```
