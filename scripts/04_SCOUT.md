## STEP 1: DEEP RESEARCH — ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS

### Most Successful Moments (Evidence-Based)
- **PMWL (PUBG Mobile World League) 2020:** Scout and his team (SouL) represented India at an international level when Indian mobile esports was largely invisible globally. The moment India showed it could compete — and it happened through Scout's clutch plays.
- **"Scout wali game" phrase:** Coined by Indian gaming community organically — a phrase meaning "plays like Scout" — the highest compliment. Organic community language is the ultimate brand marker.
- **Clutch moments compilation:** Scout's 1v4 and 1v3 situations (especially in ranked and tournaments) became defining content. The gaming community values clutch above all else — it's the equivalent of Jordan's 1988 dunk contest.
- **Stream consistency:** Scout's multi-year consistency on stream built the equivalent of a loyal army. Not viral overnight — built methodically, the way real communities form.
- **Team brand journey:** From SouL to TSM to independent — each chapter in Scout's team story added narrative complexity and kept the audience engaged.

### What Made It Work
- **Emotional Trigger:** National pride + underdog warrior narrative. When Scout succeeds internationally, it feels like India wins. The Indian gaming community desperately needed a figure who could represent them on the world stage.
- **Cultural Positioning:** Pioneer of Indian mobile esports. Not just a gamer — a category creator. The person who proved that mobile gaming could be serious, competitive, and globally relevant from India.
- **Audience Psychology:** Indian youth, 16–25, who feel gaming is dismissed by family and society. Scout is proof that gaming is a legitimate profession. They see themselves in him — which is the most powerful connection possible.
- **Key Insight:** Scout's power is the combination of **technical mastery + emotional availability**. He streams long hours, engages with his community, shows vulnerability. This human accessibility + elite skill = unbreakable loyalty.

### Visual Identity
- Aggressive orange and black (SouL era) — combat energy
- Headphones + setup aesthetic — the serious gaming shrine
- Tactical map overlays — military precision language
- High-contrast thumbnails: face close-up (emotions on display)
- Mobile device as weapon — held like a soldier holds a rifle

### Core Narrative Archetype
**The Digital Warrior / Pioneer** — Scout isn't just good at a game. He built a path that didn't exist, carried a nation's gaming ambitions on his thumbs, and proved the world wrong one clutch at a time.

## STEP 2: EXPERIENCE STRATEGY

### Concept: "The Last Alive" — A Battle Royale Inside a Website

The entire experience IS a battle royale — from the plane drop to the final circle. The user is the last player alive. Scout is their spiritual guide. Each scroll section represents a phase of the game — and a phase of Scout's career journey. **The experience literally IS the thing Scout mastered.**

Why this works:
- For the target audience (Indian gamers 16–25), the battle royale format is their native language. Starting here creates instant recognition.
- Mapping Scout's career journey to battle royale phases creates emotional resonance at every stage.
- No other gaming creator has an experience this architecturally tied to the game itself.
- The CTA (join Scout's community/watch stream) becomes the natural end of the game — a victory screen.

### SCENE 1 — "PLANE DROP: THE DECISION"
**Scroll Range:** 0–12%

**Environment:**
Inside a military transport plane. Hard edge of open cargo door. 1000m above a massive island landscape. Dawn light: purple horizon, orange sun just breaking. Wind tears through the cabin. **100 players. One beginning. One outcome.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Start: inside plane looking out the door — massive landscape below
- Lens: 20mm ultra-wide — sky and land stretch infinitely
- Slow pitch-down: camera tilts toward the ground below
- At 8% scroll: camera is pointing straight down — the freefall perspective

**Object/Character Animation:**
- 100 silhouetted characters stand at doorway
- As scroll progresses: they jump one by one in sequence
- Scout's silhouette is last — glows orange (his signature color)
- Scout jumps: camera follows him into freefall

**Transition:**
Freefall acceleration → wind distortion effect → ground rushing up → white flash landing → Scene 2

**Visual Effects:**
- Volumetric clouds (raymarching, passing through them on freefall)
- Speed distortion: radial blur increases with freefall velocity
- Wind particles: streaks past camera
- Landscape: LOD heightmap terrain, low-poly at distance, detail near ground

**Interaction Details:**
- Slow scroll: user can "choose" where to land by moving mouse (landing zone highlights)
- Fast scroll: instant freefall, cinematic rush
- Holding position at 8%: Scout hovers mid-air, city below glows with strategic markers

**Sound Design:**
- Wind, engine noise
- 100 heartbeats synchronizing
- As Scout jumps: single focused heartbeat — just his
- Freefall: wind becomes musical (pitched wind tones)

## STEP 5: TECHNICAL TRANSLATION

### Stack
```
Framework:    React Three Fiber
Terrain:      Three.js LOD + heightmap terrain
Crowds:       Instanced mesh + sprite animation
HUD:          HTML overlay (DOM) positioned via Three.js screen projection
Interaction:  Custom scroll hijacking at Scene 4 (fire button)
```

### Key Techniques

**Terrain Generation (Scene 1):**
```javascript
const geometry = new THREE.PlaneGeometry(1000, 1000, 256, 256);
// Displace vertices using heightmap
geometry.attributes.position.array // → heightmap texture data
```

**Storm Blue Zone Shader (Scene 3):**
```glsl
float distToCenter = distance(worldPos.xz, vec2(0.0));
float stormProgress = uStormProgress; // 0–1 from scroll
float inStorm = step(distToCenter, stormProgress * 500.0);
vec3 col = mix(safeColor, stormColor, inStorm);
// Add crackling electricity: noise pattern on boundary
```

**Bullet Trail (Scene 4):**
```javascript
// Trail as LineGeometry with custom shader
// Position driven by scroll/time at 0.05x speed
// MeshLine from drei with emissive material
```

**Instanced Crowd (Scene 4 + 5):**
- 10,000 instances with 32 animation frames stored in texture atlas
- Per-instance random time offset for asynchronous animation
- `InstancedMesh` with custom attribute for frame index
