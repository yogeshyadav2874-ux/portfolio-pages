# 01 — ADIDAS
## "THREE STRIPES THROUGH TIME"
### Cinematic 3D Scroll Experience Script

---

## STEP 1: DEEP RESEARCH — ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS

### Most Successful Moments (Evidence-Based)
- **Superstar (1969–present):** Started as a basketball shoe, adopted by hip-hop in the early 80s (Run-DMC's "My Adidas" 1986). First brand to sign a music act. This moment proves Adidas's power: **cultural adoption > marketing budget**.
- **Stan Smith (1971):** The world's best-selling sneaker for decades. Minimal, white, versatile — proof that Adidas wins with **restraint while Nike shouts**.
- **Impossible Is Nothing (2004):** Muhammad Ali, Laila Ali. Emotional heritage storytelling. One of the most awarded sports campaigns ever.
- **Yeezy Collaboration (2015–2022):** Kanye's Boost 350 sold out in minutes globally. Adidas weaponized scarcity + artist identity to create a $1.5B+ annual sub-brand from one relationship.
- **Originals "Original Is Never Finished" (2017):** Remixed classic art, music, culture. Showed Adidas doesn't fear deconstruction.

### What Made It Work
- **Emotional Trigger:** Nostalgia + cultural legitimacy. Adidas doesn't chase culture — it's woven into culture's DNA.
- **Cultural Positioning:** The brand that artists and athletes both choose. It lives at the intersection of **performance + self-expression**.
- **Audience Psychology:** "I'm not a follower, I have taste." Adidas buyers see themselves as culturally aware, not trend-chasing.
- **Key Insight:** Adidas wins when it remembers it's 75 years old and **leans into the archive**, not the new. Its power is in depth, not novelty.

### Visual Identity
- Three stripes (the most recognized mark in fashion)
- Black + white as the eternal foundation
- Trefoil (heritage) vs. Performance logo (sport) — two identities, one brand
- Clean European graphic design language — never cluttered
- Archive photography: grainy 35mm, real athletes, real courts

### Core Narrative Archetype
**The Cultural Bridge Builder** — Adidas is the connective tissue between sport, music, art, and street. It doesn't own a category; it owns the intersections.

## STEP 2: EXPERIENCE STRATEGY

### Concept: "The Archive Is Alive"

Not a product showcase. Not a timeline. Instead: **the user walks through a living museum where every era of Adidas culture literally assembles itself in 3D space as they scroll.**

Why this works:
- Adidas's core power is its archive depth. Most users don't know a Stan Smith is 50+ years old.
- Revealing this chronologically in 3D creates a "wow, I didn't know that" moment — the strongest emotional hook for storytelling.
- Three stripes appear in every era, proving the brand's consistency amidst cultural transformation.

## STEP 3: CINEMATIC DIRECTOR-STYLE SCRIPT

### SCENE 1 — "THE VOID BEFORE THE STRIPE"
**Scroll Range:** 0–10%

**Environment:**
Pure black void. Infinite, weightless. A single white light source at infinity — like a floodlight on an empty stage. No texture, no floor. The user feels they've entered a sacred space.

**Camera Choreography:**
- Start: Camera floating at eye level, very slight downward tilt (5°), 35mm equivalent lens
- Focal length: Long — slightly compressed, like a documentary portrait shot
- Very slow drift forward (0.002 units/frame at base scroll speed)
- NO rotation at scene start — stillness creates tension

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Three individual stripes materialize from pure light — not drawn, but *revealed*, like Venetian blinds opening
- Each stripe has a 0.3s stagger offset
- Stripes hover vertically in 3D space, slight rotation on X axis (3°) — dimensionality without complexity
- After stripes are fully visible: a 1.2s dramatic pause before any text appears

**Transition:**
Hard cut to deep darkness → stripes emerge → hold → slow dolly forward triggers next scene

**Visual Effects:**
- Bloom shader (intensity: 1.8) on stripe edges
- Subtle chromatic aberration at edges of frame
- Film grain overlay (opacity 0.04) — premium not grungy
- Depth of field: slight blur on far field

**Interaction Details:**
- Slow scroll: stripes materialize letter by letter (like typewriter but in 3D)
- Fast scroll: stripes SNAP into position with a shockwave particle burst
- Mouse parallax: stripes rotate subtly (±5°) tracking cursor

**Sound Design:**
- Absolute silence for 1.5s
- Then: single low-frequency resonant tone (A1, 55Hz) — like a tuning fork in a cathedral
- Gradually resolves into clean ambient texture

**CTA Moment:** None yet — this is the overture.

### SCENE 2 — "1969: THE COURT IS BORN"
**Scroll Range:** 10–25%

**Environment:**
The void cracks open. Hardwood basketball court materializes beneath — actual 1960s-era wood texture (worn, yellowed, authentic). Gymnasium-style pendant lights descend from above. Space feels like Madison Square Garden's underbelly. Black and white with selective color: **only the shoe is in color** (original Superstar blue/white).

**Camera Choreography:**
- Camera descends (Y: 8 → 1.2) while rotating 15° clockwise
- Arrives at a low-angle hero shot (0.4m off the floor) aimed upward at the Superstar
- Lens: 24mm wide — makes the shoe feel monumental
- Dolly: slow creep forward (classic Kubrick hallway shot energy)

**Object/Character Animation:**
- One Superstar shoe drops from above, hits the court with a **shockwave ripple** — wood floor deforms slightly (displacement shader)
- Shoe rotates 360° in 4 seconds, then settles to 3/4 angle
- Run-DMC silhouettes fade in — grainy, projected, not rendered — like archival footage projected onto 3D planes
- Text appears: "1969. Born to play. Adopted by culture."

**Transition:**
Court tiles crack like ice → beneath is a golden river of light → camera drops through → scene 3 loads

**Visual Effects:**
- Selective desaturation (everything except shoe)
- Caustic light patterns on court floor (water refraction simulation)
- Particle system: dust motes floating in gymnasium light beams
- Vintage film frame jitter on silhouettes

**Interaction Details:**
- At scroll velocity >2x normal: shoe spins faster + silhouettes multiply (3 → 6 → 9)
- Hover over shoe: it rotates to show sole detail, text shows year and cultural context

**Sound Design:**
- Basketball bouncing, slowing to silence
- Vinyl crackle → "My Adidas" bassline rises subtly (1-bar loop, low mix)
- Gymnasium ambient: crowd murmur from distance

### SCENE 3 — "THE GOLDEN EPOCH: STAN SMITH MINIMALISM"

**Environment:**
Camera emerges into blinding white space — total 180° color shift from Scene 2. **Everything is white.** Floor, ceiling, infinite horizon. One directional light source from upper left (classical portrait lighting). The space feels like inside a product visualization studio, but architectural scale.

**Camera Choreography:**
- Crane up shot: camera rises from floor level to eye level (0 → 1.7m) over 8 scroll-seconds
- Slight arc: 10° clockwise orbit around subject
- Lens shift back to 50mm — natural, humanizing
- At top of crane: brief hold (dramatic pause)

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Stan Smith rises from floor — not dropped, but grown upward like architecture
- Perfect white leather surfaces catch the single directional light (specular sheen shader)
- Green tab appears last — the only color — like a punctuation mark
- Behind shoe: infinite grid of Stan Smiths in receding perspective (instanced meshes, 200+ units)
- Text: "1971. Simplicity is a radical act."

**Transition:**
Grid of Stans suddenly shatter into green particles → particles swirl → reform as trefoil logo → expand into scene 4 environment

**Visual Effects:**
- Subsurface scattering on leather (realistic material)
- Specular highlight rolls across shoe on scroll
- Instanced Stan Smith grid: depth fog at Z>20
- Green particle burst on transition (200k particles, physics-based spread)

**Interaction Details:**
- Mouse X controls which shoe in the grid is illuminated (spotlight tracks cursor)
- Scroll pause: shoe slowly rotates on its own
- Fast scroll: particles burst earlier, shortcutting the poetry — **reward patient scrollers**

**Sound Design:**
- Clean piano, single chord
- White noise room tone

**Camera Choreography:**
- Camera positioned at far end of corridor, 1.8m high, slight downward (8°) tilt

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Yeezy 350 V2 "Zebra" materializes from geometry fragments — 3D Voronoi shatter REVERSED (pieces assemble in)

Concrete walls begin to crumble in slow motion → behind is desert landscape → wide-sky transition into scene 5

**Interaction Details:**

Open desert, dawn light. Terracotta earth, infinite horizon. A boxing ring materialized in the center — but translucent, ghostly, like a memory. The sky shifts from deep purple to burning orange. **This is the emotional core — heritage made tangible.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Aerial crane shot: starts 30m above, spirals down counterclockwise

**Object/Character Animation:**

- Particles carry residual motion — they trail light as the figure moves in slow loop
- Three stripes appear on the boxer's shorts — **only solid element in the scene**
- Text materializes word by word: "IMPOSSIBLE / IS / NOTHING"

**Visual Effects:**

- Dust particle system (10k particles, wind-driven)

**Interaction Details:**
- Scroll pause at 70%: boxer figure slowly rotates, particles trail dramatically

**Sound Design:**

**Transition:**

**Visual Effects:**

## STEP 4: ADVANCED EXPERIENCE DETAILS

### Scroll Speed Dynamics
| Scroll Speed | Effect |
|---|---|
| Very slow (0.1x) | Particles move at full fidelity; camera drifts meditatively |
| Normal (1x) | Designed experience plays as intended |
| Fast (3x) | Scenes compress; key moments flash (still impactful) |
| Very fast (10x) | Particle burst mode — everything explodes to next scene |

### Slow Motion Moments
- Shoe impact in Scene 2 (wood floor ripple) — 0.2x speed
- Boxer punch in Scene 5 — 0.15x speed (maximum drama)
- Archive implosion Scene 6 — 0.3x speed

### Dramatic Pauses
- After first stripe appearance (Scene 1): 1.2s freeze before text
- At Scene 5 at 70% scroll: experience "breathes" — a 2s pause baked into scroll progress
- Before CTA (Scene 7): 0.8s complete silence

### Key "Wow Moments" for Client Presentations
1. **Scene 2 shoe drop + shockwave** — immediate gasps
2. **Scene 3 Stan Smith grid** — the scale is unexpected
3. **Scene 5 particle boxer** — emotional and technically impressive
4. **Scene 6 archive orbit** — client thinks "I need one of these for my product"

## STEP 5: TECHNICAL TRANSLATION

### Stack
```
Framework:    React Three Fiber + @react-three/drei
Animation:    GSAP ScrollTrigger (scroll progress → Three.js uniforms)
Post-FX:      @react-three/postprocessing
               - Bloom (scene 1, 7)
               - ChromaticAberration (scene 1)
               - DepthOfField (scenes 2, 3)
               - FilmGrain (scene 2)
Shaders:      Custom GLSL
Physics:      N/A (simulated via shader math)
```

### Key Techniques

**Camera Rig:**
```javascript
// Spline-based camera path
const cameraPath = new THREE.CatmullRomCurve3([...keyframes]);
// Map scroll progress (0–1) → path parameter
const t = scrollProgress;
camera.position.copy(cameraPath.getPoint(t));
camera.lookAt(cameraPath.getPoint(t + 0.01)); // auto look-forward
```

**Selective Desaturation Shader (Scene 2):**
```glsl
// Fragment shader
vec3 gray = vec3(dot(color.rgb, vec3(0.299, 0.587, 0.114)));
float dist = distance(uv, shoeUV);
float mask = 1.0 - smoothstep(0.0, 0.3, dist);
gl_FragColor = vec4(mix(gray, color.rgb, mask), 1.0);
```

**Particle Boxer (Scene 5):**
- 500k points in `THREE.Points` 
- Position buffer animated via GPU compute (WebGPU or fallback GPGPU)
- SDF (Signed Distance Field) defines boxer silhouette — particles drift within SDF bounds

**Instanced Stan Smith Grid (Scene 3):**
```javascript
const mesh = new THREE.InstancedMesh(geometry, material, 200);
// Set per-instance transforms in a grid pattern
```

**Video Texture Stripe (Scene 7):**
```javascript
const video = document.createElement('video');
const texture = new THREE.VideoTexture(video);
stripeMaterial.map = texture;
```
