# 09 — KANYE WEST
## "THE CHURCH OF DUALITY"
### Cinematic 3D Scroll Experience Script

## STEP 1: DEEP RESEARCH — ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS

### Most Successful Moments (Evidence-Based)
- **The College Dropout (2004):** Bear mascot, soul samples, Louis Vuitton Don persona — Kanye arrived fully formed. The "College Dropout" concept (dropping out to pursue art over system) became the generation's anthem for creative ambition.
- **Graduation (2007):** Takashi Murakami cover art. Mechanical bear. Competed with 50 Cent (both released same day) — Kanye won. The album was EVENT culture before event culture existed digitally.
- **My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy (MBDTF, 2010):** Called the best hip-hop album of the decade by nearly every major publication. The George Clooney collaboration photoshoot, the excess that became art. A statement of: "Even at my worst, I am operating on a different level."
- **Yeezus (2013):** Stripped-back brutalist sound design. Performed on tour with masks and mountains and theatrical scale. Changed what a rap tour could look like architecturally.
- **Yeezy Brand:** Multiple billion-dollar shoe line. Boost 350 V2 is perhaps the most influential shoe silhouette of the 2010s. Earth tones, minimalist typography, SEASON collections that were fashion exhibitions.
- **Pablo Pop-Up Shops (2016):** Merch drops tied to album releases. First modern artist to effectively use drops-as-scarcity-marketing at city scale.
- **DONDA Listening Events (2021):** Three massive stadium events. The album as performance art. Building a house on stage. Burial performance. These events generated more coverage as events than most album releases get for music.
- **Architectural / design pursuits:** Yeezy home designs, Wyoming ranch, minimal design language across all touchpoints.

### What Made It Work
- **Emotional Trigger:** The tension between genius and chaos. Kanye's audience accepts — even desires — his contradictions, because they reflect a universal human experience of holding brilliant and broken simultaneously.
- **Cultural Positioning:** The architect of the 2000s–2020s aesthetic sensibility across music, fashion, and design. Whether you like him or not, the culture has his fingerprints everywhere.
- **Audience Psychology:** Two camps: the loyalists who follow the genius, and the critics who can't look away from the chaos. Both groups consume his content. This is his most under-rated brand achievement — making criticism itself a form of engagement.
- **Key Insight:** Kanye's most powerful creative moments happen when he **channels his duality into form**: the tender College Dropout bears vs. the brutal Yeezus masks. The stadium scale vs. the intimate album cover. **His brand IS duality, not contradiction. The website must present both sides simultaneously.**

### Visual Identity
- Earth tones + pure white + pure black (Yeezy palette)
- Minimalist brutalism: heavy serif or ultra-compressed sans-serif
- Stadium scale: nothing small, nothing quiet about his visual choices
- Religious iconography: crosses, choir aesthetics, DONDA's church-imagery
- Bears (Graduation), masks (Yeezus), angels (Donda) — mascots that evolve
- Raw industrial materials: concrete, rubber, sand

### Core Narrative Archetype
**The Visionary Provocateur / Tortured God** — Kanye genuinely believes he is a vessel for genius, and this belief — however disturbing in its real-world manifestations — has produced work that genuinely changed the landscape of American culture. The website honors the work, not the man's recent behavior.

*Note: This script focuses entirely on the creative legacy and aesthetic work. Political or harmful statements are not referenced.*

## STEP 2: EXPERIENCE STRATEGY

### Concept: "The Cathedral Is Under Construction"

The experience takes place inside an unfinished cathedral — always being built, never complete. This mirrors Kanye's creative process: every album/project is a new architectural wing. The user travels through different wings — each representing an era. **The cathedral is the art; the construction is the artist.**

Why this works:
- Kanye literally built a house on stage at DONDA. The cathedral concept is true to his own artistic language.
- An unfinished cathedral conveys: ambition that exceeds lifetime, beauty in incompletion, sacred + industrial simultaneously.
- The metaphor holds: music as architecture, eras as wings, the audience as worshippers and construction workers.
- Uniquely different from all other brand concepts in this portfolio.

## STEP 3: CINEMATIC DIRECTOR-STYLE SCRIPT

### SCENE 1 — "THE FOUNDATION: DROPOUT BEAR"
**Scroll Range:** 0–13%

**Environment:**
Deep underground. The foundation of the cathedral being poured. Concrete forms, rebar, earth around. Raw. Below a single massive column of daylight from above. In the center: the Dropout Bear — college varsity jacket, all gold, 12 feet tall. **The mascot stands where the foundation was laid.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Start: deep below, looking up at the column of light
- Camera begins rising through the construction site
- Passes the Bear at mid-level: slow 360° orbit at Bear's eye level
- Continues rising — ascending through the foundation into the main cathedral space

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Dropout Bear: still and monumental at first
- As camera orbits: Bear slowly lifts its arms in a Kanye-style raise-the-roof motion
- Bear's varsity jacket has album artwork embedded in each letter and stripe
- Concrete around Bear: pouring in real-time (fluid simulation)
- Rebar: floating in geometric formations (impossible architecture)

**Transition:**
Camera rises through foundation ceiling → emerges in cathedral nave → Scene 2

**Visual Effects:**
- Concrete fluid: SPH simulation for pouring effect
- Bear material: gold subsurface scattering + fabric simulation on jacket
- Column of light: volumetric god-ray from above
- Rebar constellation: geometric slow rotation

**Interaction Details:**
- Hover Bear's jacket letters: each one plays a 3-second clip from College Dropout
- Click Bear: it dabs (yes, genuinely — in 2004 this would have been appropriate)
- Slow scroll: concrete pours visibly around Bear's feet

**Sound Design:**
- Underground: rumble, distant construction
- Then: "Through the Wire" piano sample — warm, recognizable
- As camera ascends: soul music opening, full choir
- Reaching the cathedral: echo transforms the music

### SCENE 2 — "THE NAVE: GRADUATION / MBDTF WING"
**Scroll Range:** 13–28%

**Environment:**
The main nave of the cathedral — but rendered as a Takashi Murakami painting brought to 3D. Impossible pastel colors on cathedral arches. Murakami's flowers and cartoon sun floating as 3D geometry through sacred architecture. The floor: MBDTF album cover (the excess painting by George Clooney's referenced figure). **Where high art and pop art had a Catholic wedding.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Floating through the nave horizontally — as if swimming
- Gentle undulation: camera rises and falls on a sine wave (0.5Hz)
- Very wide lens (14mm): the architecture feels even more impossible at this scale
- Occasional 90° tilts (the world rotates): Kanye disorients intentionally

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Murakami flowers (3D petals): slowly rotating in space, no gravity
- Mechanical bear (Graduation 3D): walks along one of the arches — far up, tiny
- Cathedral windows: abstract stained glass with album artwork patterns
- MBDTF floor: the painting animates — figures in the painting move slowly

**Transition:**
The flower petals converge → become masks (Yeezus transition) → masks SHATTER → Scene 3

**Visual Effects:**
- Cartoon/PBR hybrid shader: Murakami influence (bold outlines + subsurface glow)
- Murakami flower: 10k instances, each with unique rotation
- Stained glass: emissive material casting colored light shadows
- Floor animation: vertex shader wave on the MBDTF floor plane

**Interaction Details:**
- Click any window: that album era's key songs appear
- Orbit camera: mouse controls lateral slide along the nave
- Tilt device (mobile): cathedral rotates around viewer

**Sound Design:**
- "Stronger" synth hook: filtered cathedral reverb version
- Choir echo: Kanye's voice layered with actual choir
- Flower movement: bells, light and airy

### SCENE 3 — "THE BRUTALIST WING: YEEZUS"
**Scroll Range:** 28–44%

**Environment:**
The cathedral construction becomes BRUTALIST. Raw concrete replaces carved stone. No windows — raw slits only. Industrial metal. The Yeezus masks hang on the walls — dozens of them. The space is oppressive in its deliberateness. **This is the dark wing, built during the dark period.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Handheld camera simulation: aggressive micro-shake (NOT elegant floating)
- Fast lateral movement: camera sweeps aggressively side to side
- Dutch tilts held for several scroll-seconds — the world is wrong
- Tight lens (85mm): everything is close, no room to breathe

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Yeezus masks: 40 masks hanging at different heights, all slowly rotating toward camera
- At 38%: all masks turn to face camera SIMULTANEOUSLY (horror-film precision)
- Industrial crane visible above: swinging slowly with no operator
- "YEEZUS" text in concrete — 3D extrusion, massive scale

**Transition:**
Masks screaming silently → open-mouth mask becomes a doorway → camera enters → white space → Scene 4

**Visual Effects:**
- Concrete: maximum detail PBR (water stains, form-marks, pores)
- Masks: matte white material, slight iridescence at edges
- Dutch tilt: view matrix rotation applied to camera
- Mask synchronized turn: choreographed skeletal animation trigger

**Interaction Details:**
- Hover a mask: it shows a Yeezus lyric (abstract, intense)
- Click the central mask: "BOUND 2" music video parody plays in the mouth opening (meta humor)
- Scroll fast: masks rotate rapidly — chaotic energy

**Sound Design:**
- "On Sight" industrial opening: the exact opening of Yeezus
- Metal impact sounds
- Low drone underneath
- Absolute no melody — just rhythm and industrial texture

### SCENE 4 — "THE DONDA SANCTUARY: RETURNING HOME"
**Scroll Range:** 44–62%

**Environment:**
A completely different emotional register from Scene 3. A small chapel inside the cathedral — the human scale sanctuary. Warm. Modest. A single cross. Simple wooden pews. **This is Donda's chapel — Kanye's mother, the heart that all the ambition came from.** After the brutalism, this is where humanity lives.

**Camera Choreography:**
- Very slow. Everything slows.
- Camera enters from the doorway (small doorway after the massive Yeezus space)
- Moves at walking pace toward the front pew
- Sits down (camera drops to pew height)
- Then: looks up at the cross — tilt up to ceiling

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Chapel: lit by candles only (warm flicker)
- On the altar: a photograph frame (glowing, not literal — abstracted light form)
- Choir silhouettes in the apse: singing (cloth simulation robes, breathing motion)
- The massive cathedral above visible through the ceiling — which slowly becomes transparent
- DONDA text: appears as carved letters illuminated by candlelight

**Transition:**
Ceiling becomes transparent → you see the whole cathedral from inside the chapel → it fills with white light → Scene 5

**Visual Effects:**
- Candle flame simulation: SPH fluid proxy for fluid motion
- Warm orange IBL: low intensity, intimate
- Transparent ceiling reveal: material opacity animated from 0 to 1
- Choir robes: cloth physics with breathing motion driver

**Interaction Details:**
- This scene forces slowness — scroll sensitivity reduced to 50%
- Hover choir silhouette: they harmonize louder
- The photograph frame, when hovered: "In memory of Donda West." Just that. Nothing more.
- Cannot fast-scroll through this scene — emotional respect requires time

**Sound Design:**
- "Only One" (Kanye, written as if from his mother's perspective)
- Just piano and voice — raw, not produced
- Choir underneath: barely audible, growing
- This scene: NO sound effects, NO ambient noise. Music only.

### SCENE 5 — "THE YEEZY TOWER: FASHION AS ARCHITECTURE"
**Scroll Range:** 62–78%

**Environment:**
The tallest spire of the cathedral — but now it's a Yeezy design studio. Floor-to-ceiling glass at the top. Wind at this altitude. The studio floor: SEASON collection samples (clothes arranged as architecture). Earth tones everywhere. Simple, powerful forms. **The moment Kanye applied cathedral-building logic to fashion.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Vertical ascent: elevator-like, very fast (contrast to Chapel's slowness)
- Breaks through the cathedral spire into open sky
- Studio is at the top — surrounded by open air on all sides
- Orbit: slow 360° — city below, sky above
- End position: looking out through glass at horizon

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Yeezy SEASON pieces: displayed on abstract geometric forms (no mannequins — pure shape)
- Earth tones: tan, grey, rust, cream — the palette of desert and concrete
- Yeezy 350 and 750 shoes: floating in formation at window height
- Wind: fabric simulation on garments (they billow in altitude wind)

**Transition:**
Shoes orbit → converge → form the YEEZY wordmark in shoe silhouettes → explode into particles → CTA

**Visual Effects:**
- Yeezy material: earth-tone PBR, slight fuzz simulation (neoprene/knit quality)
- Altitude wind: strong particle stream from one direction
- Garment cloth physics: Verlet simulation
- Shoe formation: instanced mesh orbital animation

**Interaction Details:**
- Hover each garment: season name + "inspiration: [reference]" tooltip
- Click YEEZY wordmark formation: shoes fly to closest wall and become a mood board
- Drag: orbit the studio 360°

**Sound Design:**
- Wind at altitude: clean, strong
- "All Falls Down" acoustic version
- YEEZY show soundtrack: minimal electronic

## STEP 4: ADVANCED EXPERIENCE DETAILS

### Scroll Speed Dynamics
| Speed | Effect |
|---|---|
| Very slow | Chapel scene extends beautifully — candles flicker in real-time |
| Normal | Designed cinematic pace |
| Fast | Nave colors flash past — psychedelic effect, accidentally appropriate |
| Very fast | Cannot fast-scroll past Chapel (speed limiter applied) |

### Slow Motion Moments
- Masks turn to face camera simultaneously (Scene 3): 0.2x — horror needs time
- Chapel candles (Scene 4): real-time (30fps only — deliberate quality reduction for intimacy)
- Donda photograph reveal: 0.1x — the most sacred moment

### Dramatic Pauses
- Dropout Bear reveals (Scene 1): 1.5s — let the scale land
- Masks all face camera (Scene 3): 2s hold — sustained discomfort
- Chapel entry (Scene 4): 3s — the longest pause in any script. Silence is the point.

### Key Wow Moments
1. **Murakami flowers in Gothic cathedral** — cultural collision that's immediately memorable
2. **All masks turn simultaneously** — horror beat in a brand experience
3. **Cathedral visible from inside the chapel** — scale perspective shift is stunning
4. **Construction sounds become music in CTA** — the conceptual payoff

## STEP 5: TECHNICAL TRANSLATION

### Stack
```
Framework:    React Three Fiber + Drei
Architecture: Procedural cathedral generation (Three.js geometry)
Cloth:        Custom Verlet solver (garments + choir robes)
Audio:        Tone.js (beat mapping in CTA scene)
Masking:      Custom stencil buffer (chapel ceiling transparency)
```

### Key Techniques

**Murakami Hybrid Shader (Scene 2):**
```glsl
// Mix PBR with toon for the Murakami look
vec3 pbrColor = computePBR(albedo, metalness, roughness, normal);
vec3 toon = vec3(floor(pbrColor * 5.0) / 5.0); // posterize
float outline = 1.0 - step(0.4, dot(normal, viewDir));
gl_FragColor = vec4(mix(pbrColor, toon, uMurakamiFactor) - outline, 1.0);
```

**Cathedral Procedural Generation:**
```javascript
// Gothic arch profile extruded along path
const archProfile = new THREE.Shape();
// ... gothic arch curve points
const archGeometry = new THREE.ExtrudeGeometry(archProfile, {
  steps: 1, depth: 0.3, bevelEnabled: false
});
// Repeat along nave with transforms
```

**Scroll Speed Limiter (Scene 4):**
```javascript
// Reduce scroll sensitivity in chapel scene
const scrollMultiplier = useMemo(() => {
  if (scrollProgress > 0.44 && scrollProgress < 0.62) return 0.3;
  return 1.0;
}, [scrollProgress]);
// Apply to GSAP ScrollTrigger's scrub value dynamically
```

**Synchronized Mask Turn (Scene 3):**
```javascript
// All 40 masks share one trigger time
const TRIGGER_PROGRESS = 0.38;
masks.forEach((mask) => {
  const targetRot = scrollProgress >= TRIGGER_PROGRESS 
    ? Math.PI // face camera
    : mask.userData.originalRot;
  mask.rotation.y = THREE.MathUtils.lerp(
    mask.rotation.y, targetRot, delta * 3 // speed of turn
  );
});
```
