## STEP 1: DEEP RESEARCH — ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS

### Most Successful Moments (Evidence-Based)
- **"Softly" (2022):** Crossed 500M+ streams. Reached international Punjabi diaspora in Canada, UK, US, Australia. The song's melancholic energy — talking about success with an undertone of pain — captured a specific generational emotion perfectly.
- **"Winning Speech" album (2022):** Certified gold/platinum. Aujla's most cohesive artistic statement. The album title itself is the positioning: he's giving the speech only winners give, but for kids from Punjab streets.
- **"Making Memories" World Tour (2023):** Sold out international venues. Proof that Punjabi music's reach is now truly global, and Aujla is its ambassador.
- **AP Dhillon Collaboration:** The AP + Karan axis became the new axis of Punjabi international music. Each amplified the other's reach.
- **Lyrical rawness:** Karan writes his own lyrics — vivid, specific, autobiographical. Lines about Ludhiana streets, struggle, money, loyalty. The specificity makes it universal.
- **Transition from ghostwriting to stardom:** He wrote for other artists for years before breaking out himself. His audience knows this journey — the "I was always there, you just didn't see me" story.

### What Made It Work
- **Emotional Trigger:** Pride + longing + validated struggle. Aujla's music says: "You went through real things. The pain was real. And you made it through." For Punjabi diaspora especially — youth between two cultures — this is intensely validating.
- **Cultural Positioning:** The authentic voice of New Punjab — not Bollywood-polished, not Western-assimilated. Rawly Punjabi but globally aware. He's the bridge between Ludhiana and London without pretending either doesn't exist.
- **Audience Psychology:** Punjabi diaspora (Canada, UK, Australia, US) + India youth. They feel: "He sounds like where I'm from, but his world is where I'm going." This is the geographic and emotional aspiration in one package.
- **Key Insight:** Aujla's career follows the structure of a ghazal — each verse (album/song) adds to one long poem about a single journey. **The narrative spine is: from anonymous dusty beginnings to gold-plated international stardom, with the heartache intact.** The website must honor both ends of this spectrum simultaneously.

### Visual Identity
- Gold and dark earth tones — wheat fields meeting luxury
- Punjab landscapes: mustard fields, narrow streets, sunset on rooftops
- Luxury aesthetics: custom cars, watches, chains — but worn as they were earned, not inherited
- Moody cinematography: underexposed, film-grain, sometimes handheld
- Typography: bold Gurmukhi mixed with clean Western sans-serif

### Core Narrative Archetype
**The Street Poet / Self-Made King** — Karan Aujla writes from streets, speaks to hearts, and fills stadiums. He is proof that authentic storytelling, told with specificity, becomes universal.

## STEP 2: EXPERIENCE STRATEGY

### Concept: "A Letter Sent from Punjab, Opened Everywhere"

The experience follows a physical letter — handwritten by Karan, sent from Ludhiana. As the user scrolls, they travel with the letter: from a cramped room in Punjab, through the sky, to venues in Canada and stadiums in the UK. **The letter is the music. The journey is the career. The landing is the global impact.**

Why this works:
- A letter is the most personal, intimate object of communication — it matches Aujla's lyrical intimacy.
- The physical journey of a letter mirrors the diasporic journey his audience has taken.
- Gold ink on dark paper is a visual language that is simultaneously humble (it's a letter) and luxurious (gold).
- Every scene transition is a postmark — another city, another destination.

### SCENE 1 — "THE ROOM: WHERE IT BEGAN"
**Scroll Range:** 0–13%

**Environment:**
A small room in Ludhiana. Pre-dawn. The kind of room where poetry is born: low ceiling, single bulb, a desk with paper and a pen, the sound of a dog somewhere outside. The room is rendered with extreme tactile fidelity — chipped paint, rough concrete, fabric curtain. **Poverty dignified by ambition.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Start outside the room, looking in through a window (slightly frosted glass)
- Slow push through the glass — camera passes through it like a ghost
- Inside: camera is very close (intimate), 35mm lens
- Scans the room slowly: desk, pen, paper, the words forming
- Ends on extreme close-up of the pen tip writing on paper

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Pen writes on paper in real time — gold ink on cream paper
- The words written are song lyrics (specific, real) — but blurred, like a moving dream
- A single tear of ink drops from the pen tip and expands into golden light
- The golden light spreads and consumes the room — transition begins

**Transition:**
Gold light dissolves the room walls → reveals open Punjab landscape → Scene 2

**Visual Effects:**
- Frosted glass transmission: custom refraction shader
- Gold ink: emissive particle trail following pen tip
- Ink tear: physics-based fluid drop + splash simulation
- Room materials: heavy ambient occlusion, scuffed PBR surfaces

**Interaction Details:**
- Slow scroll: watch every word form letter by letter
- Hover pen: the words it's writing become legible (actual lyrics)
- Fast scroll: gold explosion immediately — impatient is valid too

**Sound Design:**
- Predawn Ludhiana: dog barking far away, ceiling fan, pen on paper
- Extremely quiet — you lean in
- Gold light: single sitar note, sustained

### SCENE 2 — "THE MUSTARD FIELDS: ROOTS"
**Scroll Range:** 13–27%

**Environment:**
Punjab at dawn. Mustard fields — yellow going to the horizon. A dirt path through them. Morning mist at ankle height. A single kite flying impossibly high. **This is the landscape that made the man.** The camera is at field level — eye contact with the mustard.

**Camera Choreography:**
- Walking forward through the field — camera moves at walking pace along the dirt path
- Golden hour light from the right: rays cut through morning mist
- Occasional glances skyward (tilt up) to follow the kite
- At 22%: camera rises above the field — drone perspective, horizon reveals

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Mustard flowers sway with wind (cloth simulation, particle-level)
- Kite: thin geometry, trailing a golden tail
- Distance: abstracted silhouettes of farmers and homes
- The path in the dirt contains embedded words — lyrics burned into the earth

**Transition:**
Drone rise → horizon shows a plane in the sky → camera follows the plane → Scene 3

**Visual Effects:**
- Mustard field: particle-based instancing (500k flower instances)
- Wind: perlin noise driving rotation on each instance
- Morning mist: volumetric fog layer at Y<0.5
- Path text: emissive geometry embedded in terrain

**Interaction Details:**
- Mouse controls wind direction in the field
- Hover path text: lyrics become audible (audio clip)
- Scroll pace controls time of day (slow = golden hour; fast = full sun)

**Sound Design:**
- Morning birds
- Wind through crops: whispering quality
- A dhol (drum) in the far distance — not loud, not a beat yet, just a heartbeat

### SCENE 3 — "THE LETTER FLIES: CROSSING OCEANS"
**Scroll Range:** 27–43%

**Environment:**
Upper atmosphere. The letter (physical, envelope, Ludhiana postmark) FLIES through the sky — above clouds, near the edge of space. Below: the curvature of the Earth visible. The letter tumbles in its arc, gold ink glowing against the deep blue of upper atmosphere. **This is the music crossing oceans.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Begins behind the letter (chasing it) — letter fills the frame
- Orbits to the side: profile shot of letter in flight
- Tilts to show Earth below (geographical orientation)
- Final frame of scene: letter arriving toward a city (London or Toronto abstracted)

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Letter: detailed paper geometry, corners curl slightly in speed
- Gold ink visible through the paper (backlit translucency)
- Letter turns: shows front (Punjabi writing), back (English lyrics)
- Below the letter: time-lapse of night lights on Earth's surface

**Transition:**
Letter enters cloud cover → descends through clouds → emerges above a city → Scene 4

**Visual Effects:**
- Paper translucency: subsurface scattering on thin geometry
- Upper atmosphere: custom shader (Rayleigh scattering gradient)
- Night lights on Earth: emissive texture (NASA night map)
- Cloud layer: volumetric cloud (raymarching)

**Interaction Details:**
- Tilt device: letter banks like a plane (gyroscope response)
- Hover letter: it opens slightly — lyrics visible inside
- Scroll fast: letter accelerates — sonic boom particle effect

**Sound Design:**
- Wind at speed
- Heartbeat (now louder — music is getting closer to its destination)
- Distant "Softly" melody — played on flute, barely audible, growing

### SCENE 4 — "THE STAGE: THE WORLD LISTENS"
**Scroll Range:** 43–60%

**Environment:**
An international concert venue — not Indian-specific. Could be Rogers Centre Toronto, O2 London. Sold-out. 60,000 light sources (phone flashlights from the crowd) create a galaxy of human light. The stage floor is gold-tinted marble. **The room in Ludhiana becomes this room. The letter was received.**

**Camera Choreography:**
- Starts inside the crowd — camera at shoulder height, surrounded by people (particle crowd)
- Works its way forward: pushing through the crowd to the front
- Arrives at stage front: looking up at the stage
- Crane up: above stage looking down at the gold circle of the crowd

**Object/Character Animation:**
- Stage: dynamic lighting (moving beams, strobes at key moments)
- Microphone stand center: golden, perfect
- Aujla silhouette (particle-based, 300k points) at the mic — not photorealistic, impressionistic
- The letter from Scene 3 lands on the stage as a giant projection

**Transition:**
Crowd light → each light becomes a star → stars form constellation → constellation is Aujla's profile → Scene 5

**Visual Effects:**
- Crowd: 60k instanced light sprites (phone screens) — dynamic flickering
- Stage lighting: volumetric colored beams
- Aujla particle figure: repulsive force from audience pushes particles outward (halo effect)
- Projection: video-texture letter, gold scanline overlay

**Interaction Details:**
- Mouse over crowd: waves of light pulse from cursor
- Click stage: Aujla performs a gesture (silhouette animation)
- Scroll pause: crowd sways in unison (physics wave simulation)

**Sound Design:**
- Crowd roar — 60,000 people
- "Softly" first chorus — full production, stadium mix
- Base frequencies that vibrate (haptics if available)

## STEP 5: TECHNICAL TRANSLATION

### Stack
```
Framework:    React Three Fiber + Drei
Terrain:      Custom shader (mustard field instancing)
Fluid:        Custom Verlet ink simulation (Scene 1)
Atmosphere:   Custom sky shader (upper atmosphere)
Audio:        Howler.js with spatial audio for crowd scenes
```

### Key Techniques

**Mustard Field Instancing (Scene 2):**
```javascript
const FlowerField = () => {
  const count = 500000;
  const mesh = useRef();
  useEffect(() => {
    for (let i = 0; i < count; i++) {
      dummy.position.set(randX(), 0, randZ());
      dummy.rotation.y = Math.random() * Math.PI * 2;
      dummy.scale.setScalar(0.8 + Math.random() * 0.4);
      dummy.updateMatrix();
      mesh.current.setMatrixAt(i, dummy.matrix);
    }
    mesh.current.instanceMatrix.needsUpdate = true;
  });
};
```

**Paper Translucency Shader (Scene 3):**
```glsl
// Simulate light passing through paper
vec3 backLight = normalize(uLightPos - vWorldPos) * -1.0;
float transmitted = max(dot(vNormal, backLight), 0.0) * uTransmission;
vec4 frontColor = texture2D(map, vUv);
gl_FragColor = mix(frontColor, vec4(uBackColor, 1.0), transmitted * 0.4);
```

**60k Crowd Lights (Scene 4):**

// Each point is a phone flashlight
