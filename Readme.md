## ORIGINAL BRIEF

You are a senior brand strategist, cultural researcher, and cinematic experience director.

Your task is to perform **deep research and analysis** on the following brands/creators and then generate **high-end cinematic, scroll-based 3D website scripts** based ONLY on what has actually worked for them historically:

Brands / People:

* Adidas
* Puma
* Jordan (Nike Air Jordan)
* Scout (Indian gamer)
* One additional top gamer (choose based on impact)
* Samay Raina
* Karan Aujla
* Meek Mill
* Kanye West
* Rawme Hood

---

## STEP 1: DEEP RESEARCH (MANDATORY)

For EACH brand/person, analyze:

1. Their **most successful products / campaigns / moments**
2. What made them successful:

   * Emotional triggers (hype, rebellion, luxury, relatability, etc.)
   * Cultural positioning (streetwear, premium, underground, etc.)
   * Audience psychology
3. Their **visual identity and aesthetic language**
4. Their **core narrative archetype** (e.g., rebel, innovator, underdog, icon)

DO NOT give generic summaries. Extract **actionable insights**.

---

## STEP 2: TRANSLATE INTO EXPERIENCE STRATEGY

For EACH brand/person:

* Define a **single powerful concept** for a 3D immersive website
* The concept MUST be rooted in real insights from Step 1
* Avoid generic ideas like “show products” or “cool animations”

---

## STEP 3: CINEMATIC DIRECTOR-STYLE SCRIPT (VERY DETAILED)

For EACH brand/person, generate a **scroll-based cinematic script** with:

### Structure:

* 5–7 scenes per brand
* Each scene must include:

1. Scene name
2. Scroll range (e.g., 0–15%)
3. Environment description
4. Camera choreography (position, movement, lens feel)
5. Object/character animation
6. Transitions between scenes
7. Visual effects (particles, lighting, shaders, etc.)
8. Interaction details (scroll velocity effects, micro-interactions)
9. Sound design suggestion (optional but preferred)
10. Final CTA moment

---

## STEP 4: ADVANCED EXPERIENCE DETAILS

Also include:

* How scroll speed affects animation
* Where to use slow motion vs real-time
* Where to use dramatic pauses
* Key “wow moments” designed to impress clients

---

## STEP 5: TECHNICAL TRANSLATION (IMPORTANT)

For EACH concept, briefly mention:

* Suggested stack (Three.js / React Three Fiber / GSAP)
* Key techniques (camera rig, shader effects, instancing, etc.)

---

## OUTPUT STYLE:

* Think like a **film director + creative agency pitch**
* Avoid generic or beginner-level ideas
* Make it feel like **₹5L–₹10L level premium work**
* Prioritize **originality + storytelling + brand alignment**

---

## CRITICAL RULES:

* Do NOT hallucinate random campaigns—base insights on widely known brand successes
* Do NOT repeat the same concept across brands
* Each brand must feel **completely unique**
* Focus on **why the idea works**, not just what happens

---

Your goal is to give me script and produce output so strong that it can directly be used in a **premium portfolio to attract high-paying clients**.
